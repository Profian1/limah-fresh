B2B (Corporate & Institutional): Offices, hospitals, schools, government bodies (e.g., KNEC, Posta Kenya, KAA) requiring bulk bowser water delivery, dispenser maintenance, and regular bottle refills.

2. Key Features & Functionality
E-Commerce Aesthetic UI: Clean product grids, badge labels (e.g., "KEBS Certified"), volume selectors, and product detail cards.  
PDF

WhatsApp Direct Ordering: Clicking "Order via WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message format (e.g., "Hello Limah Fresh, I would like to order [Product Name], Quantity: [X], Location: [Location]").

"Request a Quote" System: Dynamic modal form for bulk bowser orders, custom water dispenser contracts, or dispenser maintenance.  
PDF

Trust & Compliance Badges: Prominent display of KEBS Diamond Mark of Quality, KRA Excise Licensing, and Ministry of Health Certifications.  
PDF

Future-Proofing: Codebase architecture prepared for M-Pesa Express API integration in Phase 2.
